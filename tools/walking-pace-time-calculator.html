Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate walking time from distance and pace?

Multiply distance by pace per mile or kilometer. A 3 mile walk at 20 minutes per mile takes about 60 minutes before stops, crossings, hills, or route delays.

How do I calculate walking pace from distance and time?

Divide total walking minutes by distance. A 5 kilometer walk completed in 55 minutes has an average pace of 11 minutes per kilometer.

Is this the same as a running pace calculator?

The formula is similar, but this page uses walking examples, walking-friendly pace ranges, and links to step planning tools.
